# ClassWallet

> ClassWallet is a patented digital wallet and public-funds disbursement platform used by government agencies, school districts, and program administrators to distribute, control, and reconcile public money. Recipients spend allocated funds online or in-store while administrators get spending controls, approved marketplaces, reimbursement workflows, direct vendor payments, and audit-ready reporting and compliance. It powers K-12 education programs, Education Savings Accounts (ESAs), scholarships and grants, government assistance, and vocational rehabilitation.

This profile is an API Evangelist network company record. ClassWallet publishes product, program, and support surfaces but no public developer API or developer portal at this time.

## Products
- [Digital Wallet](https://classwallet.com/digital-wallet/): Managed digital wallet for distributing and spending public funds.
- [DirectPay](https://classwallet.com/directpay/): Direct payments to vendors and service providers.
- [Marketplace](https://classwallet.com/marketplace/): Approved vendor marketplace for compliant fund spending.
- [Reimbursement](https://classwallet.com/reimbursement/): Reimbursement submission and approval workflows.

## Solutions
- [Government Agencies](https://classwallet.com/government/): Public-funds distribution and compliance for agencies.
